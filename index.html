01 — About

Engineering with the flow of water

Welcome! I'm Sebastián Contreras Aracena, a Civil Engineer specialized in Hydraulics with more than 3 years of experience in hydrology, hydraulic modeling and environmental permitting for mining and infrastructure projects in Chile.

I currently work at DLS Binar Ltda., a consulting firm delivering hydraulic and geotechnical studies for the mining industry. I've contributed to projects for major clients such as Codelco and Spence BHP, producing hydraulic structure designs, hydrologic calculations and technical reports for water-sector permits (DGA).

My technical background spans rainfall frequency analysis, 1D and 2D hydraulic modeling (HEC-RAS, Iber), flood mapping, and the design of hydraulic structures such as culverts and channels. I'm passionate about engineering solutions that balance functionality, sustainability and regulatory compliance.

Wayra Wind Farm — Environmental Impact Assessment

The Wayra Wind Farm is a new wind + solar generation plant with battery storage, located in the Taltal Wind Reserve Zone (Antofagasta Region) on a property awarded by tender from Bienes Nacionales. It will deploy 48 wind turbines of up to 8 MW each (384 MW total) plus three solar PV areas (North, Northeast and East) totaling 173 MWn — a project capacity of up to 557 MW, plus battery storage of up to ~750 MWh, all injected into the National Electric System (SEN) via medium-voltage underground lines, the project's step-up substation, and a ~26 km high-voltage line connecting to the expanded Parinas substation.

Goal: build and operate a 557 MW non-conventional renewable generation plant (with up to ~750 MWh of battery storage) injected into the SEN, harnessing wind in the reserve zone and contributing to Chile's energy demand and matrix diversification.
